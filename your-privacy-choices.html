Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor the Global Privacy Control signal as a valid opt-out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising for the browser that sends it. You don't need to do anything else. The signal overrides any earlier "Accept all" choice in that browser.

Other privacy requests

Depending on where you live (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Nevada, Oregon, Texas, Virginia, Washington and other states), you may have the right to:

  • Know / access the personal information and consumer health data we have about you, and get a copy;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your information;
  • Withdraw consent to collection or sharing of consumer health data;
  • Get a list of the third parties and affiliates we've shared your consumer health data with;
  • Appeal a decision we make about your request.

We won't discriminate against you for using these rights. Send a request with the form below, or email privacy@glp1reviewguide.com. We don't have user accounts, so we'll verify your request by matching the email address or other details you give us to our records. We'll confirm we received it within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why (up to 45 more days).

Authorized agents

An authorized agent can submit a request for you. We may ask the agent for signed permission from you, and may ask you to confirm your identity with us directly, unless the agent has a valid power of attorney.

What "sale" and "sharing" mean here

We don't sell personal information for money. But some state laws define "sale" and "sharing" broadly. They can include letting advertising partners set cookies or pixels on our pages to measure ads or show ads across other sites. That's what this opt-out turns off. We don't sell consumer health data, and we don't use sensitive personal information for anything beyond what the law allows, so we don't offer a separate "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link. See our Privacy Policy, Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for the full details.

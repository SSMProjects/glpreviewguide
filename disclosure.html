4GLP-1 medications and FDA status

The Site covers GLP-1 receptor agonist medications, including semaglutide and tirzepatide. Their regulatory status depends on how they are made and distributed.

Brand-name medications

Wegovy® and Zepbound® are FDA-approved for chronic weight management. Ozempic® and Mounjaro® are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and are sometimes prescribed off-label for weight loss. FDA approval means a medication has been reviewed for safety and effectiveness through clinical trials for its approved uses.

Compounded medications

Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved. They have not been reviewed by the FDA for safety, effectiveness or quality, and they are neither brand-name drugs nor generics. They are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies, but state licensing is not the same as FDA approval. The FDA has declared the national shortages of semaglutide and tirzepatide resolved, and compounded versions are now under increased regulatory scrutiny.

All GLP-1 medications, brand-name or compounded, require a prescription from a licensed healthcare provider. Ask your provider whether any GLP-1 medication is appropriate for your medical history and goals, and which pharmacy prepares it.

5Side effects and risks

GLP-1 medications can cause side effects. Commonly reported ones include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, abdominal pain and reduced appetite. Less common but more serious risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney problems and severe gastrointestinal reactions.

Boxed warning: FDA-approved labeling for GLP-1 receptor agonists includes a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors. These medications should not be used by people with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).

This is not a complete list of side effects. It is based on publicly available FDA labeling and medical literature and is provided for information only. Individual experiences vary. If you have side effects from any medication, contact a licensed healthcare provider right away.

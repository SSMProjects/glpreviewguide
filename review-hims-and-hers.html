Medical care

You complete an online intake form, and a medical provider decides whether a prescription is appropriate. Members get unlimited messaging with a provider after that.

Pricing

The weight-loss membership costs $39 for the first month, then $149 a month. Medication is billed separately, starting around $149 a month for an oral GLP-1 starting dose. Prices were checked in September 2026; confirm current pricing on the provider's site.

Who it's best for

People who want to choose between several brand-name pills and pens. Read the cancellation steps before you sign up.

Our verdict

A familiar wellness brand offering several brand-name oral and injectable GLP-1s, with unlimited provider messaging and lifestyle guidance. A licensed clinician decides whether a GLP-1 medication is appropriate, and results vary from person to person.

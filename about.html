GLP1ReviewGuide.com is an independent publisher that compares online weight-care programs offering GLP-1 medications. Our goal is simple: give you a clear, honest picture of what each program includes and what it really costs, so you can walk into your consultation informed and ready to ask the right questions.

Why we exist

Telehealth weight-care programs have multiplied quickly, and on the surface many of them look alike. The real differences tend to sit in places most people never reach: an introductory price that rises after the first month, medication costs that climb with every dose increase, membership fees billed separately, or cancellation terms that lock you into a full year.

Comparing programs properly means working through pricing pages, terms of service, FAQs and sign-up flows one by one. That takes hours, and most people simply don't have them. We do that work and present what we find in a consistent, side-by-side format.

How we research

Every program we cover goes through the same process. We evaluate programs as services. We don't take the medications ourselves or publish personal weight-loss results.

  1. Gather the public facts

    We collect each program's published pricing, medication options, clinician model, state availability and refund and cancellation policies directly from its own website and terms.

  2. Walk through the sign-up

    We go through each program's intake flow to see what it asks, when you're charged, and whether the price shown at the start matches the price at checkout.

  3. Compare long-term cost

    We look past the first month to the ongoing monthly price, including how it changes as your dose goes up and what is billed on top of the membership.

  4. Score on fixed criteria

    Each program is rated against the same four weighted criteria, so scores can be compared fairly across providers.

  5. Review and recheck

    Health information is checked against FDA prescribing information and peer-reviewed research, and a medical reviewer reads our content before it's published. We recheck prices regularly and update pages when programs change.

What we are, and what we're not

It matters to be clear about our role. We help you compare the programs that provide care. We do not provide care ourselves.

We are

  • An independent consumer publisher
  • Researchers of pricing, policies and program features
  • Committed to correcting errors quickly and openly

We are not

  • A healthcare provider, clinic or pharmacy
  • Able to prescribe, assess eligibility or give medical advice
  • Affiliated with or endorsed by any medication manufacturer

How we make money

GLP1ReviewGuide.com is free to read. We earn revenue through affiliate partnerships with some of the programs we cover: if you visit a provider through one of our links and sign up, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you.

These relationships never determine our scores. Every program is rated on the same published criteria, and providers cannot pay to improve their rating or remove unfavorable information. Full details are in our advertising disclosure.

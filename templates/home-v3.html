Common questions

How does the quiz pick a program?

It maps your answers to the program that scores best for your stated priority — cost, insurance use, speed, or structure — based on our independent editorial scoring. It's a starting point, not a prescription.

Do I need insurance?

No. Several programs we rank, including our top pick, offer flat cash pricing with no insurance required. Others are best run through existing coverage — we note which on each review.

How do you stay independent if you earn commissions?

We may earn a commission when you start a program through our links. That can affect placement, but the editorial score is set independently and doesn't change based on any partnership.

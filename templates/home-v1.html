Frequently asked

How do you make money if the rankings are independent?

We earn a commission when readers start a program through our links. That compensation can affect where a partner appears, but it never changes the editorial score — the score is set before any commercial relationship.

Do I need insurance to use these programs?

No. Several of the programs we rank, including our top pick, offer flat cash pricing with no insurance required. Others work best when run through existing coverage — we note which is which on every review.

How often are prices updated?

We re-check pricing and offers monthly, and each review page carries a "last updated" date so you can see how current it is.

Is this medical advice?

No. This site is for general information and comparison only and does not replace advice from a licensed clinician. Any treatment decision should be made with a qualified healthcare provider.

Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to compare their options, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

On this page

  1. Our goal
  2. What we've done
  3. Known limitations
  4. Get help

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. We test new pages before we publish them and review the site at least twice a year.

What we've done

  • Text and interface colors meet AA contrast ratios.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, including the quiz, the cookie banner and the privacy controls, and focus is always visible.
  • Pages use real headings, landmarks and labels, and include a "skip to content" link.
  • Images have text alternatives. Charts and score bars are also given in text.
  • Layouts reflow to 320px wide and support 200% zoom without losing content.
  • Animations respect your "reduce motion" setting.

Known limitations

Some provider logos and third-party pages we link to aren't under our control and may not meet the same standard. If something here gets in your way, please tell us.

Get help or give feedback

Email accessibility@glp1reviewguide.com and tell us the page and the problem. We aim to reply within 2 business days and can send information in another format.

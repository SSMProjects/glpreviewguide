The short version

We're a comparison publisher, not a healthcare provider. We don't ask for your medical history, weight, diagnoses or prescriptions. The "Find your fit" quiz runs in your browser, and its answers aren't sent to us or saved. Advertising and analytics tools that could reveal your interest in weight-care programs stay off unless you turn them on. We never sell consumer health data.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to consumers in Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, and anyone else covered by a consumer health data law. It is operated by [Legal entity name] ("GLP1ReviewGuide.com," "we," "us"). It supplements our Privacy Policy. If the two conflict, this policy controls for consumer health data.

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. That includes information that shows you are seeking health care services, and inferences drawn from other data.

3. Sources

  • You, when you contact us or submit a request.
  • Your browser or device, through cookies, pixels and similar technologies, when you allow them.
  • Advertising partners, who may tell us that you arrived from one of our ads (for example a click ID). We only receive this when you allow advertising cookies.

4. Why we collect and use it

  • To show you the page and quiz result you asked for (necessary to provide the service).
  • With your consent: to measure which guides are useful (analytics) and which ads bring visitors (advertising measurement).
  • To earn referral commissions, by letting a provider know a visit came from us when you click a provider link.
  • To answer your messages and requests, keep the site secure and meet legal obligations.

We don't use consumer health data to decide whether you're eligible for anything, and we don't build health profiles about individuals.

6. Your consent

Beyond what's necessary to provide the site, we collect consumer health data only with your consent, and we share it only with a separate consent. You give or refuse both in our cookie banner. You can change your mind at any time in or on Your Privacy Choices. Withdrawing consent stops future collection and sharing from that browser.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access that data;
  • Get a list of all third parties and affiliates we've shared it with, and how to contact them;
  • Withdraw consent to its collection and sharing;
  • Have it deleted. We'll also tell our processors and the third parties we shared it with to delete it;
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.

To make a request, use the privacy request form or email privacy@glp1reviewguide.com. We'll verify it's you in a commercially reasonable way. Because we don't keep accounts, we'll usually do this by matching your email address. We respond within 45 days, and may extend by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice. Requests are free up to twice a year.

8. Appeals

If we refuse your request, you can appeal within 45 days by emailing privacy@glp1reviewguide.com with the subject "Privacy appeal." We'll give you a written decision and our reasons within 45 days. If the appeal is denied, we'll tell you how to contact your state attorney general. For Washington residents, that's the Washington Attorney General's Office.

9. Security and retention

Access to consumer health data is limited to staff and processors who need it. We use encryption in transit, access controls and vendor contracts that restrict use. We keep contact messages and request records for up to 24 months, then delete them. Analytics data is kept for [14] months. Advertising cookies expire according to the Cookie Policy.

10. Things we don't do

  • We don't use geofences around health care facilities to identify, track, collect data from or send messages or ads to people.
  • We don't sell consumer health data.
  • We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

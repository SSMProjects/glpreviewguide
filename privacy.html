1. Who we are

GLP1ReviewGuide.com is an independent publisher operated by [Legal entity name], [street address, city, state ZIP]. We compare online weight-care programs. We are not a healthcare provider, pharmacy or insurer, and we don't prescribe or sell medication.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email and message when you contact us or make a privacy request.
  • Device and usage data, collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring site and approximate (city-level) location. Beyond what's needed to deliver the page securely, we collect this only if you allow analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Ad and referral identifiers: click IDs from ad platforms, and referral IDs we attach when you click a provider link.
  • Quiz answers: processed in your browser to show a result, and not sent to us or stored.

We don't collect Social Security numbers, payment details, medical records, prescriptions or insurance member numbers. Please don't send them to us.

3. How we use information

  • To run, secure and improve the site;
  • To measure traffic and ad performance, with your consent;
  • To earn commissions by letting partners know a visit came from us;
  • To respond to messages and privacy requests;
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Sharing, "sale" and targeted advertising

We share information with:

  • Service providers (hosting, security, email, analytics), under contract;
  • Advertising platforms ([Google Ads, Taboola, Outbrain]), only if you allow advertising cookies;
  • Telehealth partners we link to, who receive a referral ID when you click;
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or a buyer if the business is sold.

We don't sell personal information for money. Some state laws may treat advertising cookies as a "sale," "sharing" or "targeted advertising." You can opt out on Your Privacy Choices, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out.

5. Consumer health data

Some information about visits to a health-topic site can be "consumer health data" under laws in Washington, Nevada and Connecticut. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains what we collect, who receives it and your rights.

7. Your rights

Depending on your state, you may be able to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information; opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling; withdraw consent; and appeal our decision. Submit a request on Your Privacy Choices or email privacy@glp1reviewguide.com. We'll verify your request, respond within 45 days, and won't treat you differently for using your rights. Authorized agents may submit requests with your written permission. To appeal a decision, reply to our response with the subject "Privacy appeal." If we deny the appeal, you can contact your state attorney general.

8. Notice for California residents

In the past 12 months, we collected these categories: identifiers (such as IP address and email), internet activity, approximate geolocation, inferences (interest in weight-care programs), and sensitive personal information (only as it may be inferred from visiting health pages). We collected them from you, your device and ad partners, for the purposes in section 3.

If you allowed advertising cookies, we disclosed identifiers and internet activity to advertising platforms, which may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. We haven't sold or shared the information of anyone we know to be under 16. We use sensitive personal information only for purposes permitted under California regulations (section 7027(m)), so we don't offer a separate "limit" link.

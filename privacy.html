1Introduction

GLP1ReviewGuide.com is operated by GLP1 Review Guide ("GLP1ReviewGuide.com," "we," "us" or "our"). We respect your privacy and aim to be clear about how we handle information. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect when you visit our website (the "Site"), why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices available to you. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

United States users. The Site is intended for people located in the United States. We do not direct our services to users outside the United States.

HIPAA notice. We are not a healthcare provider, health plan or other HIPAA-covered entity, and we do not collect protected health information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. Telehealth providers you may visit from our Site are responsible for their own HIPAA compliance.

Not medical advice. The Site is an informational comparison service. We do not provide medical advice, prescribe or sell medication, or determine eligibility for treatment.

2Information we collect

We collect a limited amount of information, described below. In the past 12 months, we have collected the categories listed here.

Information you provide to us

When you contact us by email or submit a privacy request, we collect the information you choose to share, such as:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your state of residence (for privacy requests); and
  • the content of your message.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain technical information is collected. Beyond what is strictly needed to deliver pages securely, this is collected only if you allow analytics or advertising cookies:

  • IP address and approximate (city-level) location;
  • browser type and version, device type and operating system;
  • pages viewed, time spent on pages and links clicked;
  • the website or ad that referred you to the Site.

Ad click identifiers and referral IDs

If you reach the Site from a Google ad, the web address includes a Google click identifier ("GCLID") and may include campaign details such as campaign, ad group, creative, keyword, match type, device, network, placement and target IDs. Unless you have opted out (see sections 8 and 9), we keep these in your browser's session storage for the rest of your visit and add them, along with a referral ID, to the tracking link when you click through to a program. This happens even if you haven't turned on analytics or advertising cookies, because it is how we are credited for referrals and how we measure which ads lead to sign-ups. If you have opted out, we pass only the referral ID.

Information we do not collect

Answers to our "find your fit" quiz are processed in your browser to show you a result and are not sent to us or stored. We do not collect Social Security numbers, payment card details, medical records, prescriptions, diagnoses or insurance member numbers. Please don't send this kind of information to us.

3Cookies and tracking technologies

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. We and our partners use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to keep the Site working, measure traffic and, with your permission, measure and improve our advertising.

Only strictly necessary cookies are active by default. Analytics and advertising cookies are off until you choose to turn them on in our cookie banner. You can change your choices at any time in . You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly. Our Cookie Policy lists the cookies we use and how long they last.

Browser storage. Besides cookies, we use your browser's session storage to hold ad click identifiers for the length of your visit. Session storage clears when you close the tab or window. We also use a first-party cookie to remember your privacy choices. Our Cookie Policy lists these items.

4Analytics

If you allow analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are popular and how people arrive. This helps us improve our content. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. In addition to our cookie settings, you can opt out of Google Analytics across websites by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site;
  • measure Site traffic and advertising performance, with your consent;
  • let our partners know when a visit or sign-up came from our Site, so we can earn commissions;
  • attribute referrals and sign-ups to the ads that led to them, including reporting conversions back to Google Ads (unless you opt out);
  • respond to your messages and process privacy requests;
  • detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse, security incidents and technical problems; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not use your information to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

When you click a link to a telehealth provider or other program listed on our Site, you leave our Site and go to that provider's website. Providers are independent companies. Any information you give them, including health information, is collected and used under their privacy policy and terms, not ours.

We don't control, and aren't responsible for, how providers collect, use, store or protect your information, or for their compliance with HIPAA or other laws. We encourage you to read each provider's privacy policy before sharing personal or health information with them.

7How we share information

In the past 12 months, we have disclosed information to the following categories of recipients, for the reasons described:

Who we share information with
  • Service providers that host, secure and maintain the Site, deliver email and provide analytics. They are bound by contract to use information only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising platforms, such as Google, Taboola and Outbrain. Their cookies and pixels load only if you allow advertising cookies, and help us measure which ads lead to visits and sign-ups. Separately, unless you opt out, Google receives the ad click identifier from your visit together with a record that a referral or sign-up occurred, when our affiliate tracking platform imports conversions into Google Ads.
  • Our affiliate tracking platform, Everflow, which runs our tracking links (go.glp1reviewguide.com). It records clicks, receives the referral ID and, unless you opt out, the ad click identifiers described in section 2, and matches them to sign-ups reported by the programs and affiliate networks we work with.
  • Telehealth partners we link to, which, directly or through their affiliate networks, receive a referral ID (and, unless you opt out, ad click identifiers) when you click through so they can credit the visit to us. We do not send them your name, email or quiz answers.
  • Legal and safety recipients, such as courts, regulators or law enforcement, when we believe disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect our rights, our users or others, or to prevent fraud.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred to the new owner, subject to this policy.

8Sale, sharing and targeted advertising

We do not sell personal information for money. However, some state privacy laws define "sale," "sharing" or "targeted advertising" broadly enough to include advertising cookies that let ad platforms measure or personalize ads. To the extent our use of advertising cookies falls under those definitions, you have the right to opt out.

Passing ad click identifiers to Everflow and, through conversion imports, to Google may also be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. This happens by default when you click through to a program from a Google ad, and stops if you opt out by any of the methods below.

You can opt out at any time on our Your Privacy Choices page, by turning off advertising cookies in , or by using Global Privacy Control (see below).

9Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

Global Privacy Control (GPC). We honor GPC. If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising, our advertising tags will not load for that browser, and we won't pass ad click identifiers from that browser. You don't need to do anything else.

Do Not Track (DNT). The older Do Not Track browser setting has no agreed legal meaning, so we don't respond to it separately. If you want to limit tracking, we recommend enabling GPC, which we honor.

11Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access / right to know: ask what personal information we have collected about you and how we use and share it.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Portability: receive a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out: opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling.
  • Withdraw consent: change your cookie choices or withdraw consent at any time.
  • Appeal: appeal our decision if we decline to act on your request.
  • Non-discrimination: we won't treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

12Exercising your rights

How to submit a request

You can submit a request by:

  • using the request form on Your Privacy Choices; or
  • emailing privacy@glp1reviewguide.com. California residents may include "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

To opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising, you can also use our cookie settings or Global Privacy Control. No request is needed.

Verifying your identity

To protect your information, we need to verify your identity before acting on access, correction or deletion requests. We will ask you to confirm your name, email address and state of residence and to respond from the email address you used. Because we hold very little information about visitors, we may not be able to link a request to any data if you haven't contacted us before. Opt-out requests don't require verification.

Authorized agents

You may authorize an agent to submit a request on your behalf. We will ask the agent for a valid power of attorney or written permission signed by you, and we may ask you to verify your identity directly with us.

Response times and appeals

We respond to most requests within 45 days. If we need more time, where the law allows, we will tell you why and may extend this by up to 45 additional days.

If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our response with the subject line "Privacy appeal." We will respond to appeals within the time required by your state's law, typically 45 to 60 days. If we deny your appeal, you may contact your state attorney general.

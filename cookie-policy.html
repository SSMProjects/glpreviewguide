What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. "Similar technologies" include pixels, tags, local storage and SDKs that do the same kind of job. We call all of these "cookies" here.

Our default: non-essential cookies are off

Because this site covers a health topic, analytics and advertising cookies stay off for everyone until you choose to turn them on in our cookie banner. We also treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a "no" to advertising cookies.

How to control cookies

  • On this site: use (also in the footer of every page) or Your Privacy Choices.
  • In your browser: block or delete cookies in its settings. If you delete cookies, we'll ask for your choices again.
  • Industry tools: Google ad settings, DAA WebChoices and NAI opt-out.

Affiliate links

When you click a provider link, the provider's own site may set its own cookies to record that you came from us. That's covered by the provider's privacy policy, not ours. We pass a referral ID only, never your quiz answers.

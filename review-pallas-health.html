Medical care

Intake, clinician review, check-ins and refills all happen online, and review is often same-day. Prescriptions are written by US-licensed clinicians through an affiliated medical group, and the medical team is led by a physician board-certified in obesity medicine. Most states need no video visit; 7 states plus Washington, D.C. require one to start.

Pricing

There's no membership fee. Compounded semaglutide is $139 for the first month, then $597 every 12 weeks (about $199 a month) or about $159 a month on the annual plan. Compounded tirzepatide is $179 for the first month, then about $299 a month, or $259 a month on the annual plan. Nothing is charged until a clinician prescribes, and you're refunded in full if they decide treatment isn't right for you. Brand-name medications are cash-pay and billed monthly. Prices were checked in September 2026; confirm current pricing on the provider's site.

Who it's best for

People paying out of pocket who want one predictable monthly price, including dose increases, and the choice between compounded and brand-name medication. If you want to use insurance, or prefer a company with a longer track record, look at #2 and #3.

Our verdict

All-in GLP-1 pricing with no membership fee, the same price at every dose, and both compounded and FDA-approved brand-name options. A licensed clinician decides whether a GLP-1 medication is appropriate, and results vary from person to person.
